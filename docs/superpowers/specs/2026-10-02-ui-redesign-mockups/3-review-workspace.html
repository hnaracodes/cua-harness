Crowding: two steps on the same spot

Scores are label bands plus an offset, so two steps can sit at exactly the same point, where no amount of zoom pulls them apart. Pick how ties behave (zoom/pan exists in both).

A. Stack & fan out (recommended)

Overlapping badges merge into one "×2" badge; click fans them out with leader lines to the real point. A loop includes or excludes the stack together (they really are at one spot); to split them, approve/remove one from the fan or the list.

B. Shrink to dots + popover list

Crowded badges shrink to small numbered dots and never move; hovering a cluster lists what's in it. Positions stay perfectly literal, but small dots are harder to read and to click.
