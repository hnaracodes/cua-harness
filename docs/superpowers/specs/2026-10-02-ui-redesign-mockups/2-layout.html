A. Chat left, workspace right (recommended)

Like Claude's artifact panel or ChatGPT Canvas. The canvas gets ~60% of the window, so drawing has room and pan/zoom feels natural. During the run, the right side becomes a live view of the agent's desk while the chat narrates.

B. Everything in one chat column

Purest chatbot feel: the plan arrives as a message containing a small grid; you expand it to a full-screen overlay to draw. Simplest mental model, but the canvas is cramped until expanded, and the drawing gesture is one click further away.

C. Separate full-screen stages

Chat home → a dedicated full-window review workspace (biggest canvas, steps beside it) → back to a chat thread for the run. Maximum drawing room, but the conversation disappears while you review, so “revise this step” has to happen in the workspace.
